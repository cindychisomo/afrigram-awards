How it works

From nomination to the gala in ten days.

  1. 1 October, morning

    Nominees announced

    Nomination is free. Nominated businesses are notified and can start gathering support by asking followers to comment on their nominee post and tag their account.

  2. 1 October, 5:00 PM

    Top 3 finalists announced

    Our judges select three finalists per category in each country.

  3. From 1 October evening to 8 October, 7:00 PM

    Public voting

    Every finalist gets an official poster on our Instagram page. Like and comment on your favorite to vote.

  4. 9 October

    Finalists fly into Kigali

    Each finalist business travels to Rwanda for the gala.

  5. 10 October

    The awards gala

    Category, country and All-Africa winners are crowned.

  6. 11 October

    Finalists return home

    Safe travels to every finalist.

All times are Tanzania time (East Africa Time).

Questions

How do I vote?

Open our Instagram page, find the official poster of the finalist you support, then like it and leave a comment. Every genuine like and comment counts toward the public vote.

Is it free to be nominated?

Yes. Nomination is free, and there is no fee for businesses that are not selected as finalists.

What do finalists pay?

Finalists pay a one-time, non-refundable confirmation deposit of USD 100 (TZS 260,000 for Tanzanian finalists). It secures their spot and comes with an economy round-trip flight to Kigali for the owner or a representative, two nights of hotel accommodation, and an official finalist poster.

Who can take part?

Businesses that sell on Instagram in Tanzania, Kenya, Rwanda, Nigeria, Ghana or Zambia, in one of the seven categories.

When and how are prizes paid?

By bank wire or mobile money, within 14 business days after the event.

Who organizes the awards?

Cairotech Solutions, to show how technology has helped small businesses grow into big brands.
